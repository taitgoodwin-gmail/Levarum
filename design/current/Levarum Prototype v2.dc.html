HOW THE BACKEND WORKS HERE
Unlocking writes a submission to on-device storage (a stand-in for the real database). The operator console reads that store as an inbox — booking a call flips the record's status. Claude generates each draft approach from the intake answers and picks a real-world workflow per pain from the solution catalog (Claude, Zapier, RingCentral, Cal.com, Calendly, Stripe, QuickBooks, HubSpot, Twilio, Make…). Operators can swap options; effort and tooling cost roll up automatically. If Claude is unreachable, a deterministic draft is used and labelled. Email and scheduling are simulated.
