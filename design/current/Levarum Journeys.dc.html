WORKFLOW AND DECISION TREE

Two tracks, one handoff.

Everything starts at the home page. The customer track runs in petrol, the admin track in rust, and the two shared states where one hands to the other are neutral. Every decision, terminal state and drop-off is marked.
STEP
Something happens. Solid card, coloured left rail.
DECISION
A fork. Diamond glyph, tinted ground, branches named.
TERMINAL
The track ends here, on purpose. Filled dark.
DROP-OFF
He leaves. Dashed. Each one names its recovery.

The handoff — shared states

CUSTOMER → ADMIN
Submit writes the record
The email at the gate is the trigger. One record — business, hours band, pains, hours back, slot if picked — lands in the store and shows in the inbox as New straight away.
Prototype: device store. Live: formEndpoint POST → same shape.
ADMIN → CUSTOMER
The call is the only thing he feels
Nothing in the admin track is visible to him except this. Moving a card to Booked is what turns into a phone ringing — see Terminal 1 on the customer track.
Gap: no automatic confirmation to him when status changes.

Admin track

2 decisions · 2 terminals · 1 drop-off.
ENTRY · SIGN IN
Signs in from the footer link
Not in the public nav. Prototype credentials only — this gate is a demo, not security.
STEP · INBOX
Newest submission first
Each card carries the whole intake: business, hours band, pains picked, hours back, slot asked for, email. Enough to make one call without opening anything else.
DECISION 1
Is this one qualified?
Owner-run, five or more hours a week on back office, and at least one pain from the five. Two of three is a judgement call; one of three is not.
YES
Into the status chain
Contact within one working day, or the plan goes cold.
TERMINAL 1 · NOT A FIT
Marked Done, no call
He keeps the plan. Nobody wastes a call. Gap: no “not a fit” status — it looks the same as finished work.
STEP · STATUS CHAIN
NEW → CONTACTED → BOOKED → DONE
Four states, one direction, and one legal reverse: Booked back to Contacted when a call falls through. The status persists on the device between sessions.
DECISION 2
Did he respond?
YES
Booked → the call
The one moment the admin track becomes visible to him.
NO · LOOP
Stays Contacted
Two more attempts, spaced. The card does not move, which is the point — it keeps showing up.
DROP-OFF · GONE QUIET
Three tries, nothing
Recovery today: none. Needs a decision — park it, or one note in ninety days.
TERMINAL 2 · DONE
Call held, build agreed or not
Both tracks end here. Everything after this — the build, the handover, the two weeks of fixes — is work, not website, and belongs on a different map.

Partner track

Slim by design. Never crosses the customer path — footer entry only, petrol not rust, no competing CTA.
ENTRY · FOOTER STRIP
Sees the partner line
Low on Home, inside the footer band. A business owner scrolling past it loses nothing.
STEP · PARTNER INTAKE
Four fields
Name, what they do, where they plug in, email. No deck, no call booking — the ask is smaller than the customer ask on purpose.
HANDOFF · SHARED
Lands in the same inbox
Written to the same store with kind: partner, so it shows as PARTNER · New and counts in its own tile.
STEP · SAME CHAIN
New → Contacted → Booked → Done
Deliberately the same four states. A second workflow would be a second thing to forget.
DROP-OFF · NOT NOW
Terms not ready
Recovery today: none, and honestly stated on the page — early, few clients, terms open.
