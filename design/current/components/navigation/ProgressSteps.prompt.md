Intake progress. Not interactive and not focusable — the step is always also stated in words.

```jsx
<ProgressSteps step={2} total={3} note="About 90 seconds in total" />
<ProgressSteps step={2} form="bars" />
```

Complete steps are rust, pending are `--lv-line`. Sits directly above the step's `h1`, with `--lv-g-2` beneath it.
