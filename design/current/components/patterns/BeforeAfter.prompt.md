The brand's central editorial device, and the thing that does the work an icon set usually does. Dashed grey left rule = the broken way today. Solid petrol left rule = the same moment once it is set up.

```jsx
<BeforeAfter
  beforeLabel="A BATHROOM FITTER TODAY"
  before="Job done Friday, invoice typed Sunday at the kitchen table, nobody chases it, paid six weeks later."
  afterLabel="THE SAME WEEK, SET UP"
  after="He taps the job done on the way to the van. Invoice out that minute, reminders at one week, two and three. Sunday stays Sunday." />
```

Write both halves as one concrete scene with a named trade — a roofer on a ridge, a woman with no hot water at nine at night. The "today" half is quiet ink; the "set up" half is full ink. Never sell in the after copy; just describe what happens.
