The one-thing-to-do-next block. Appears exactly once per plan.

```jsx
<FixFirstCard title="Invoice automation"
  why="It pays for itself first and nothing else depends on it, so it is the safest place to start." />
<FixFirstCard tone="tinted" title="Invoice automation"
  why="It pays for itself first and nothing else depends on it." />
```

The `why` is always about sequencing — what unblocks what — never about savings. Use the outlined form inside a plan and the tinted form inside an example card.
