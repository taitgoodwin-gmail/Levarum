Three questions, a plan, one short call.

No discovery workshop, no proposal document, no six-week engagement before anything useful exists.

HOW THE PIECES STRING TOGETHER

One missed call, end to end.

Seven stations on one line, using tools you already pay for. Nothing here needs you at a keyboard.
1
The call comes in your phone number
Your existing number, the one on the truck. Nothing changes for the caller.
2
Missed
Four rings, no answer, because you are under a sink. Today this is where the job dies.
3
Text back text messaging
Within seconds she gets a text in your voice with your next two open slots and a link.
4
Booked your calendar
She taps a slot. It blocks your calendar and the drive time around it.
5
Reminded text messaging
A confirmation the day before asking for a yes or a no. A no puts the slot back on the market before you have lost the morning.
6
Invoiced QuickBooks
You mark the job done. The invoice goes out on the spot, with the right line items and the right address.
7
Chased, then paid QuickBooks
Unpaid at one week, two and three gets a polite nudge. Paid ends the line and nothing else needs doing.
THE SHAPE IS THE SAME
A dental desk starts the same line at a booking form and ends it at a recall. A salon starts it at a walk-in. The stations move; the idea does not.
WHAT IS NOT ON THIS PAGE
How each station is actually wired. That is the work you are paying for, and it stays our side. What you see here is where your week comes back.
